## Ascension upgrades (A8 / A9)

Unlike vanilla bosses (where ascension only adds HP), bosses in this mod get **extra upgrades at Ascension 8 / 9**:

- **Upgraded cards** — the cards named here become **upgraded** (suffix "+") at that level; everything else is unchanged;
- **Extra relics** — some bosses carry **one more relic** (card names and relic names are mixed in the columns below);
- **Minion HP** — summons (Osty, the spawn, the minions of the Minion Regent, …) switch to a higher HP bracket **from A8 on**;
- **The boss's own HP does not scale with ascension** — only with act + Neow's Blessing (see the HP formula above).

## Dominion of each character (Dread mode)

In Dread mode each act carries the dominion of **that act's main boss character**; it only applies in **normal rooms** of that act
(never in boss rooms). Some dominions **scale with the act** (different effect in act 1/2/3), some are the same every act.

| Character | Dominion effect | Scales with act? |
|---|---|---|
| Ironclad | At the start of combat every enemy gains **1 stack** of Demon Form | No |
| Silent | At the start of combat you lose **1 / 2 / 3** Strength and gain **1 / 2 / 3** Weak | **1/2/3 by act** |
| Defect | Every enemy gains **1 / 2 / 3** Artifact (**on top of** their own Artifact; **in multiplayer it also grows with player count**: +1 at 2 players, +2 at 3) | **1/2/3 by act** |
| Regent | At the start of your turn, turn 1 non-status / non-curse card into **Dazed / Slimed / Debris** | **1/2/3 by act** |
| Necrobinder | At the start of your turn, 1 random card in hand costs **+1** this turn | No |
| Fool | At the start of combat, add **2 / 3 / 4** Sadness to your draw pile | **1/2/3 by act** |
| Watcher | At the start of combat every enemy gains **2 stacks** of Devotion; at the start of your turn each of them gains **2 Mantra**; at 10 Mantra it spends 10 and enters Divinity, tripling its attack damage that turn | No |

> A dominion is **not** a boss's "special effect": a special effect only applies inside that boss's own fight, a dominion only in normal combats of that act. The two never overlap.
